📱 Best Way to Open on Android

  1. Open the Salesforce app on your phone
  2. Tap the Search icon (🔍) at the top
  3. Tap the QR / Barcode scanner icon in the search bar
  4. Point your camera at the QR code on the report
  5. The record will open directly in the app
or use the buttons above

⚠️ If "Open in App" does not work

  1. Go to Android Settings → Apps → Salesforce
  2. Tap Open by default
  3. Turn on Open supported links
  4. Return here and tap Open in App again

📱 If Salesforce did not open automatically

  1. Tap Open in App above
  2. If prompted, tap Open to confirm
alternative method

📷 Using the Salesforce App Scanner

  1. Open the Salesforce app
  2. Tap the Search icon (🔍)
  3. Tap the QR / Barcode scanner icon
  4. Scan the QR code on the report

🖥️ Redirecting to Salesforce

  1. You will be redirected to Salesforce automatically
  2. Log in if prompted
  3. The Assessment page will open for this record
